🔄 Insights Sync
Last synced: — Date range: —
Volume, Pace and Quality come from the Insights PFA dashboard, using whatever date range is set there. Volume is stored as the cumulative total, so logging a raise still resets it to zero — and once a raise is on file, Pace and Quality are measured from that date too (points ÷ days worked since; passes ÷ checks since). A metric a card doesn’t report is left untouched, not zeroed. Turn auto-sync off to edit numbers by hand.
Removed from the roster
People you have taken off the roster. They stay hidden and every Insights sync ignores them. Restore puts someone back on the next sync — their current metrics return, but any raise log or saved evaluation from before they were removed does not.
Added from Insights — needs role and pay
People Insights reported who had no row in this app. They are now on the All Staff tab with a starter role and no pay rate — set both in the roster above. Hub Managers and Service Leads are not listed here; they are scored on their hub’s metrics.

TECHNICIAN II EVALUATION
Scoring context for TX2: Technician II (Intermediate): Held to a higher bar on responsibility and ownership. Should handle repairs and Lightspeed workflows independently, surface issues proactively, and demonstrate initiative. No formal leadership expectation yet.

Evaluation categories
Attendance & Punctuality High ×3
Consistently on time and reliable. At TX2, their presence directly affects shop throughput — scheduling issues should be communicated early.
Attitude & Professionalism High ×3
Models composure and professionalism. Cultural tone-setter — how a TX2 shows up affects the whole shop floor.
Teamwork & Support High ×3
Actively supports peers, picks up extra work when needed, contributes to shop flow beyond just their own work queue.
Build Proficiency Med ×2
Executes builds efficiently and accurately. Comfortable with accessory installs and firmware updates with minimal errors or rework.
Forest & Lightspeed High ×3
Handles both Forest (asset/workflow tracking) and Lightspeed (POS, work orders, payments, labor recording) accurately and independently. Both are TX2 responsibilities per R&R.
Repair Quality High ×3
Performs mechanical and electrical repairs on customer and fleet bikes following shop standards for diagnostics, safety, and turnaround times. Core TX2 responsibility per R&R.
Responsibility & Ownership High ×3
Owns their work end-to-end without being followed up on. Accountable for the quality of output, not just completion.
Initiative Med ×2
Identifies shop floor issues and addresses them without being told. Doesn't wait for a lead to call out something that needs fixing.
